You have everything you need. You have been told this. Beyond the path, something asks whether that is enough.

I · The beginning

The Garden

Order and obedience

Our journey begins in Eden. What kinds of freedom are possible within a world whose rules are given?

How should we understand freedom in this setting?

II · A voice in the leaves

The Whisper

Satan’s influence

We ask how Satan’s influence may be understood in Paradise Lost and how influence appears in Westworld. These are interpretive questions, not settled claims.

When does another person’s suggestion become your own desire?

III · The turning point

The Bite

Knowledge and deliberation

The fruit marks a turning point in the story we trace. We ask how knowledge and deliberation are represented without assuming that eating the fruit establishes a single account of consciousness.

Does knowing more make you freer?
